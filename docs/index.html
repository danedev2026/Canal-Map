Free · Offline · No ads · No tracking
Open the live map →

A beautiful, fully offline map of the UK's connected navigable network — canals and the navigable rivers narrowboats actually cruise — for boaters. Everything is bundled in the app, so it keeps working with zero signal for days at a time.

What it does

  • Nationwide offline map of canals & navigable rivers
  • Locks, water points, Elsan, pump-out, refuse, pubs and moorings — with clear icons
  • Numbered canal bridges and place names
  • Live Canal & River Trust stoppages & closures (with an offline fallback)
  • Route planning with distance, lock count and estimated journey time — save routes for later
  • Search, “near me”, GPS follow-me and a tidal-hazard overlay
  • A descriptive boat-movement log you can export as CSV or GPX — handy for continuous cruisers
  • Optional online aerial imagery, light & dark themes, and a safety guide

Use it on desktop

No phone to hand? Open the live map in your browser to pan and zoom the whole network, see the facilities and place names, search, and check live stoppages — the same offline map, right in the page.

Distances and journey times are estimates, and tidal waters are hazardous — always check current conditions and official notices before setting off.

Free, and private

No adverts, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking. The only thing the app ever fetches is a small daily stoppages file; the map itself is always read from your device. See the privacy policy.
