Everything you need on the cut

Built for narrowboaters and continuous cruisers — the whole connected network, bundled and ready with zero signal.

Nationwide offline map

Every connected canal and navigable river, rendered from your device — no data needed, ever.

🧭

Route planning & rings

Tap two points or pick a classic cruising ring, and get distance, lock count and estimated time. Save routes or export them.

⚠️

Live stoppages & notices

Daily Canal & River Trust closures and restrictions, searchable and shown on the map — with an offline fallback.

🛟

Facilities & moorings

Locks, water points, Elsan, pump-out, refuse, pubs and moorings — clear icons, tidal-hazard overlay, numbered bridges.

📄

Boat log & exports

A descriptive movement log for continuous cruisers, exportable as CSV or GPX — plus GPX/PDF for routes.

🔒

Free & private

No adverts, no accounts, no analytics. Light & dark themes, a safety guide, and your data stays on your device.

Plan on the big screen

No phone to hand? The live map runs right in your browser — pan and zoom the whole network, search, plan a route with a printable PDF plan, and check today's stoppages.

Free, and private

No adverts, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking. The only thing the app ever fetches is a small daily stoppages file; the map itself is always read from your device. Read the privacy policy.

Distances, lock counts and journey times are estimates, and tidal waters are hazardous — always check current conditions and official notices before setting off.
