Privacy Policy

Canal Map: UK Waterways · last updated 26 July 2026

Short version: the app collects nothing about you. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no data is sent to us or anyone else.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts and includes no analytics or advertising software. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or device identifiers.

Location

If you choose to use the “my location”, “near me”, “route from here”, or “log my position” features, the app reads your device’s GPS location. This is used only on your device to show your position, sort nearby places, plan a route, or add an entry to your on-device boat log. Your location is never sent off the device. Location permission is optional and only requested when you use one of those features.

Your boat log

Positions you log are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. They are never uploaded. You can export them to a file yourself (for example to prove continuous cruising) and delete them at any time; removing the app deletes them.

Network use

The only information the app fetches over the internet is a public file of Canal & River Trust navigation notices (stoppages), downloaded from a static web address. No personal data, location, or identifiers are sent with this request. If you are offline, the app uses the last saved copy. Standard server logs (such as your IP address) may be recorded by the file host (GitHub) as part of normal web hosting; we do not control or use those logs.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the app’s GitHub repository.
